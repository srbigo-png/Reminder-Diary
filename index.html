What the app does

Say it, it remembers

Every text field has a microphone. Tap it and speak your reminder instead of typing it.

It does not give up

At the time you set, the reminder fills the screen, even on a locked phone, and is read out loud until you stop it.

It all stays with you

No account, no sign-up, no server of ours. Your reminders are stored on your phone only.

Creating a reminder

The title is the only thing you must fill in. Everything after it is there for the reminders that repeat: the days, the times, and when it all stops.

When the reminder goes off

This is the part the app was built for. A reminder you do not notice is a reminder that did not work.

Lists by voice

One reminder can carry a whole list. You speak the list in one go instead of typing it line by line, and the same flow works for Shopping, Health, Family and General.

Questions that come up

The reminder did not appear at night. Why?

Most likely it fell inside your quiet hours. In that case it is not deleted, it is held until the morning. You can change the range in Settings.

My phone is on silent. Will I hear the reminder?

Yes. The reminder plays at alarm volume, so it is heard on silent and on Do Not Disturb as well.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Creating reminders, the reading, the dictation and the lists all work fully offline. A connection is needed only for the purchase itself.

A pill three times a day, what is the right way to set it?

One reminder with a daily repeat, and two more times inside "Extra times per day". Not three separate reminders. That way you can also edit or stop all of them together.
